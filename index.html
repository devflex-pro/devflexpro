How DevFlex works

DevFlex focuses on systems where the backend is the product: event ingestion, tracking, routing, reporting, integrations, workers, and operational APIs. The work usually starts with data flow, business rules, storage, and reliability requirements rather than visual polish.

Open-source products help show the direction of the engineering work, while custom development handles the parts that are specific to a team: traffic sources, advertiser APIs, partner logic, campaign dimensions, payout rules, deployment constraints, and internal workflows.
