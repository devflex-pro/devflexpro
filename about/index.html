About DevFlex

DevFlex is a technical product studio focused on open-source products and custom backend development.

Contact DevFlexOpen source

What DevFlex is

DevFlex is built around a simple idea: useful software usually starts with a real operational problem. The focus is on products and custom systems where traffic, events, conversions, campaigns, notifications, integrations, and reporting need careful backend design.

The studio combines open-source work with private development. Open-source projects make the engineering direction visible, while custom systems handle the business-specific logic that cannot be copied from a generic platform.

Focus areas

  • AdTech systems
  • Affiliate marketing platforms
  • Traffic tracking and routing
  • Conversion processing
  • Analytics backends
  • Push and automation workflows
  • High-load backend infrastructure

Engineering approach

Simple architecture

Systems should be understandable, maintainable, and built around real operational needs.

Backend-first development

DevFlex focuses on data, APIs, workers, events, integrations, and production behavior.

Practical product thinking

Product decisions should support the workflows teams actually run every day.

Open-source when possible

Open code makes technical evaluation faster and creates useful foundations for custom systems.

Custom systems when needed

When generic tools are not enough, DevFlex builds around your business logic.

What matters in the work

DevFlex keeps the implementation close to the workflow: how data enters the system, how it is validated, how operators use it, how reports are generated, and how integrations fail or recover. This keeps technical decisions tied to product behavior.

The goal is not to make software look larger than it is. The goal is to build systems that are direct, inspectable, and useful for the teams responsible for running them.

If you need a custom backend, AdTech platform, tracking system, or product MVP, contact DevFlex.
