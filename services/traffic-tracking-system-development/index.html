When a custom tracker makes sense

Generic traffic tracking tools are useful until the workflow becomes specific: private routing rules, custom offer logic, internal approval flows, unusual attribution needs, or reporting that must match how the business actually buys and monetizes traffic.

A custom traffic tracking system can keep click, campaign, offer, source, and conversion data in one controlled backend. This is useful for affiliate teams, media buying teams, AdTech startups, and operators who need infrastructure that can be changed without waiting for a closed platform roadmap.

Typical requirements

Custom routing logic

Route traffic by source, campaign, geography, device, offer status, or business rules.

Reliable conversion data

Collect conversions, deduplicate events, and connect postbacks to clicks and campaigns.

Private infrastructure

Keep tracking, reporting, and campaign data inside your own controlled system.

Engineering approach

DevFlex treats tracking as backend infrastructure first. The important parts are predictable redirect behavior, clear data models, fast write paths, reliable conversion matching, and reporting queries that remain understandable as campaigns and traffic sources grow.

The implementation can start small and stay modular: click ingestion, routing, postbacks, campaign management, reporting, and integrations can be designed as separate parts of one system instead of a hard-to-change monolith.
