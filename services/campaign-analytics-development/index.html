Why custom analytics helps

Campaign analytics becomes difficult when the data lives across traffic sources, trackers, affiliate systems, advertiser callbacks, spreadsheets, and internal tools. Teams need one reporting model that explains what happened across clicks, conversions, revenue, cost, payouts, and operational changes.

A custom campaign analytics platform can reflect the metrics and dimensions that matter to the business instead of forcing every workflow into a generic dashboard. This is useful for affiliate teams, media buyers, product teams, and operators who need fast answers from high-volume event data.

Stack fit

DevFlex works with backend-heavy analytics systems using Go, ClickHouse, MongoDB, Redpanda, Docker, APIs, and event-driven architecture where appropriate.

The backend can be designed around ingestion, normalization, aggregation, and query APIs, with dashboards built on top of a reporting model that is stable enough for daily use and flexible enough for new campaign dimensions.

Good analytics infrastructure also needs operational details: data freshness, failed imports, delayed postbacks, attribution changes, and export consistency. These details are often the difference between a dashboard people look at and a system teams trust.
